FAQ

Frequently Asked

How do I get access to GHOST-HUNT products?
All products require authorized-use verification. Click "Request Access" on any product card to begin the enterprise onboarding process. Our compliance team reviews each request before granting access. This ensures all deployments are governed by our responsible-use framework.
Are these tools available as open source?
The Agent Harness Suite is available as open source on GitHub. Framework licenses (GHOST-HUNT, JADe) require a research or enterprise license. All tools are designed for research laboratory environments and authorized assessments only.
What is the responsible-use governance framework?
Every product includes a mature responsible-use framework with authorized-use verification, enterprise compliance review, and detection engineering runbooks. We focus on defensive resilience and assessment methodology, not public tool distribution.
Can I use these frameworks for academic research?
Yes. The Agent-Driven Corpus Linguistics framework and Autonomous Research Loop are designed for academic research. The JADe framework has been validated through published replication studies on independent corpora (CLMET, 40M tokens) with close quantitative agreement.
What agent profiles are available in GHOST-HUNT?
GHOST-HUNT includes three specialized agent profiles: HERMES (rapid tactical relay and low-latency communication), OPENCOE (navigation and automation in complex operational environments), and FREEBUIFF (experimental parameters for expanding AI research limits). All profiles include JSON and Markdown configuration interfaces.
